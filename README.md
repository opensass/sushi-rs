## 📜 Intro

**Sushi RS** is a **highly customizable**, **accessible**, and **zero-dependency** SVG sushi component library for WASM frameworks including **Yew**, **Dioxus**, and **Leptos**.

## 🤔 Why Use Sushi RS?

1. **🎨 Four Shapes**: `Circular`, `Square`, `Triangular`, and `Oval` sushi silhouettes.
1. **📷 Two Views**: `Top` (bird's-eye) and `Front` (isometric) perspectives.
1. **🥗 Rich Ingredients**: Salmon, Tuna, Avocado, Egg, Caviar, Cucumber, Crab, Shrimp, Mango, Cream Cheese, Wasabi.
1. **💥 Exploded View**: Deconstructs every component side-by-side with labels and animations.
1. **🎀 Top-Edge Decorations**: Red/Black Caviar, Sesame, Herbs, Tobiko rings on the rim.
1. **🧩 DRY Architecture**: A single `src/svg.rs` engine powers all three framework adapters.
1. **♿ WCAG Compliant**: `role="img"`, `aria-label`, `<title>` on every SVG group, full keyboard navigation.
